# Support

- **Questions and ideas:** [Voyage Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/voyagesync/discussions).
- **Bugs and feature requests:** The **Issues** tab of the repository concerned.
- **Security reports:** Follow the `SECURITY.md` of the repository concerned. Never report vulnerabilities in public.
- **Code of conduct reports:** Email **conduct@team.voyage**. See `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.
- **Anything else:** contact@team.voyage

The maintainers aim to respond within 7 days. There is no paid support at the moment.
